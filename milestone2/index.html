Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

The site will feature information regarding mental health services offered (such as anxiety and depression counseling), an "About Me" section for my mother’s professional background, contact information, and specific details about her Southern Utah office location.

Where will you get all of the content for your site (ie. images & text)?

I will interview my mom to write the professional bios and service descriptions. Photos will be original shots of her office space and high-quality landscape photography of the Southern Utah area to create a calming atmosphere.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Mental health is important, therefore I should help people and assist my moms busness

Why should a site be built for this?

most people look for healthcare providers online first. A professional website establishes credibility, provides 24/7 access to information, and makes it easier for new clients to find a therapist.

What would you like users to do at your site?

I want users to feel a sense of peace and hope, learn about the therapy process, and ultimately use the contact form or phone number to schedule their first consultation, or if possible a portal to schedule automatically via zoom or in person.

What is the goal of the site?

The primary goal is to grow my moms therapy practice by connecting her with local residents who are seeking professional mental health support.
