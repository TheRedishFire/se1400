Competitive Analysis

Analyzing other local therapy practices in the Southern Utah area.

What they are doing right:

  • Many competitors offer secure telehealth options for remote sessions.
  • They use calming, muted color palettes that feel appropriate for mental health.
  • Some have downloadable intake forms to save time before the first visit.

What they are doing wrong:

  • Several websites look extremely outdated and are not optimized for mobile phones.
  • They lack a warm, personal connection. The "About" pages are often too clinical and stiff.
  • Insurance and pricing information is frequently hidden or confusing to navigate (this is the most important issue id like to solve to be as transparent as possible)

How we can do it better:

We will build a mobile-responsive, modern website that feels welcoming and safe. We will include a personal, empathetic bio for my mom so clients feel like they know her before they even walk in. We will also include a clear, easy-to-read FAQ section detailing pricing, insurance, and what to expect in the first session. We might also include some reviews.
