Target Audience Demographics

To build an effective website, we need to know exactly who we are advertising to. Below is the breakdown of my moms ideal clients.

Geolocation

Our primary audience lives in Southern Utah, specifically focusing on the St. George, Washington, and Cedar City areas. We will also cater to the broader state of Utah and beyond via telehealth or zoom

Age Range

The primary users of this site will be adults whom want to better their mental health. excluding children

Gender

While therapy is for everyone, the majority of the website users scheduling the initial appointments will likely lean Female. Research shows women are often the ones initiating therapy, however gender is entirely irrelivant

Other Identifying Demographics

  • Occupation: Working professionals, stay-at-home parents, and local university students (e.g., Utah Tech University).
  • Needs: People experiencing anxiety, depression, life transitions, or relationship stress.
  • Tech Fluency: Comfortable using smartphones to browse, fill out forms, and schedule appointments online, however there will be an option to call for older clients.
