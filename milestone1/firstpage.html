Intro

Benefiting from the support of my community,

Paragraph1

I've sought to contribute as much as possible. From middle school into high school, I immersed myself in various forms of community engagement, including volunteering and service projects, participation in multiple varsity athletics, and leadership in school clubs like SGA and DECA. Alongside balancing multiple part-time jobs and challenging myself academically with honors and AP coursework, these experiences not only helped me overcome early childhood traumas but also inspired me to embrace a mindset of continuous learning and personal growth.

Paragraph2

My non-traditional upbringing also fostered a strong sense of independence in me, which led me to forego my admission to the University of Tennessee in my hometown and move to Utah on my own in the fall following high school graduation. Recognizing higher education as the cornerstone of a fulfilling life, I chose to embark on this journey to create my own path to success, free from any constraints of adversity that have previously plagued my family and others close to me.
