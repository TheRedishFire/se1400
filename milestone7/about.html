My Therapeutic Philosophy

I truly believe that every individual has the innate ability to heal and to find inner peace. Life can bring profound challenges that obscure this inner strength, leaving us feeling lost, overwhelmed, or stuck. My job as a therapist is to assist in helping individuals find the tools they already possess, and to provide unwavering support for this process.

Therapy is not a one-size-fits-all experience. I am open to providing significant direction or gentle nudgings, depending on what you are ready for and what best suits your personal journey. Together, we will create a safe, non-judgmental space where you can explore your thoughts and emotions freely.

Who I Work With

My ideal client is one that simply has the courage and desire to change. Taking the first step to seek therapy can be daunting, but it is also an act of profound bravery. I work best with abuse issues, depression, anxiety, relationship issues, and grief. Over my 26 years in practice, I have had extensive experience working with individuals, couples, and adolescents.

I frequently assist clients in dealing with a range of complex issues, including post-traumatic stress, childhood abuse, sexual issues, and the difficult transitions associated with divorce.

A Safe, Empathetic Space

I have found some clients were initially embarrassed or felt shame at some of the feelings they had. It is completely normal to feel apprehensive. You will find me to be open and accepting to all feelings and thoughts from within. One of my greatest strengths as a clinician is the ability to be empathetic and see the positives in people, even when they struggle to see it in themselves.
